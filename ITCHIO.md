## Title

Scopetta

## Short description

Italian Scopa with four opponents and six classic decks. Play in your browser, on Windows or Android.

## Long description

Pull up a chair for a game of Scopa.

Capture cards, collect the coins, claim the settebello, and clear the table for a scopa. Each game is one deal: a compact contest against an opponent with a character of their own.

Choose from **Graziano, Franco, Valerio, and Piero**, and play with six decks: **Trevisane, Romagnole, Napoletane, Piacentine, Francesi, and Bresciane**.

- **Play in your browser**, or download the Windows and Android versions for offline play.
- **Italian and English**, with a language choice in Settings.
- **Rules included**, ready to consult while learning.
- **History and settings saved on your device.**
- **Free to play**, with no account required and no in-game advertising.

Traditional cards, green felt, and one more smazzata.

**Android installation:** If you have version 1.0.3 or earlier, uninstall it before installing this version. Uninstalling deletes your local settings and history.

**Windows installation:** The download is a portable, unsigned executable. Windows may display a SmartScreen warning; installation details are included in the release notes.
